🛡️ Ethical Governance Guardrails

Workload Risk scores represent operational system bottlenecks and workload distribution pressures. They are strictly designed to trigger managerial re-balancing, sprint scope adjustments, and wellness support. Under Infosys governance policies, this index is never a diagnostic assessment of employee capability, motivation, or medical health, and is never used for punitive disciplinary actions.
